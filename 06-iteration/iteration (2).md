## What the evidence says

_What real usage showed: numbers if your tool has analytics, counted behaviour if it does not. Put the signal that matters on screen._

- **Primary signal:** 5.6 page views per visit
- **What moved:** Customer Service users want to understand how to reduce churn by selecting specific accounts and taking actions and thus high page visits to understand the score and model results.
- **What didn't:** 65% bounce rate suggests that workflow might not be inutitive.

_Analytics snapshot: visitors 15; page views 84; views per visit 5.6; duration 1 min 18 seconds; bounce 65%._
