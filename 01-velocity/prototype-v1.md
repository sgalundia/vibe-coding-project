## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup, their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

- **What they understood immediately:** Internal tool not being used as expected
- **What confused them:** No indication of how to interpret the health score. Not able to read the value. Interpretation is hard factors that are leading to churn.
- **The assumption they thought you were testing:** The churn factors are assumptions such as low seat activation and providing recommendations for that. It does not really show the validation with respect to low seat activation and what it means by that.
- **The gap between what you intended and what they read:** The churn factors are not clear and not validated with real data for users to understand. It provides the distribution of churn factors but user does not feel confident about the churn factors.
